# Licensing FAQ

## Can I read and clone it without buying?
Yes, for inspection and purchase assessment, preserving notices. Search engines and
AI assistants may also discover and briefly summarize it with attribution. Public
GitHub viewing and forks remain subject to GitHub's platform terms. This does not grant
arbitrary downstream project use.

## Can a company use it internally for free?
No such exception is granted. Project use requires the applicable product or written
commercial license, including prototypes and nonprofit/research uses unless separately
authorized. Mandatory legal exceptions remain unaffected.

## Must existing Unity customers pay again?
No. A valid purchase's EULA controls already granted rights. Appropriate seats/entities
and contractor rights still matter. Buying one product does not buy both.

## Is AI training possible if paid?
Yes, with express separate written permission for specified materials and uses. A game
asset purchase is not automatic permission to train a model. No training dataset or
shipped model payload is present here.

## Does this bind GitHub to pay for training?
No. GitHub Terms D.4 grant platform/affiliate AI rights; J.3's opt-out concerns AI-feature
input/output and expressly does not cancel D.4-D.8 rights over public repository content.
The publisher accepted public hosting with this disclosed boundary. A different host
must be assessed under that host's own terms; self-hosting changes platform control,
not the technical possibility of scraping.

## Why not BSL, MIT, FSL or a Noncommercial license?
MIT grants broad reuse. BSL has an automatic change-license deadline of at most four
years. FSL also transitions and permits broad noncompeting uses. A noncommercial grant
would allow uses the publisher wants to license. This custom review license contains
no automatic open-source conversion and no general free project-use grant.

## Does this establish ownership of all low-precision algorithms?
No. Source expression, contractual grants and product authorship are separate from
exclusive rights over ideas. Publication/hashes alone do not establish patent priority
or novelty. Independent implementations and mandatory legal exceptions cannot simply
be prohibited by asserting ownership of mathematics.
