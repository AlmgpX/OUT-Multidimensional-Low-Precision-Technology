# Third-party boundaries

No Unity engine assemblies, NUnit binaries, native binary plugins, external animation
banks, Mixamo content or Quaternius sample data are distributed in this snapshot.
Unity/NUnit references in source describe external dependencies, not included software.
OUT Motor's separately delivered standard animation bank has its own CC0 provenance;
that bank and its third-party samples are deliberately excluded here.

Algorithms such as XPBD, quantization and interpolation have established prior work.
References do not imply ownership or implementation of an entire cited research system.
Third-party rights, where applicable, are not relicensed by the repository's LICENSE.
