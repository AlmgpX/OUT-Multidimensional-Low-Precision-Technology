# OUT FIBER changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-18

- Close-up default play with 256 inhabitants; larger 1024 × 768 m world and debug atlas.
- 128 houses with real doors, beds and interiors; daily needs, professional schedules and actual sleep.
- 20 original Tales characters, biographies, secrets, dialogue, pockets and all four original quests.
- Inventory, trade, faction consequences, witnessed crime, bounded peer ties and rumors.
- Synthetic daily-life/social model refits; 23,168-byte inference bank, frozen after startup fitting.
- Near/mid/far and sleeping update cadences, swept coarse movement, selectable full 60 Hz AI stress mode.
- Finite resource gathering, tools, six camp object types, watering/growing, food crafting and camp stockpile.
- Same-build checksummed local saves for actors, inventory, quests, resources and camp.
- Pixel sprites, day/night lighting, roof sleep counts, bounded blood and Unicode UI.
- License identifier is the first line of every published source/script/build file; publication audit enforces it.
- Versioned tests, timing reports and screenshots; v0.1 evidence retained under benchmarks/v01.
